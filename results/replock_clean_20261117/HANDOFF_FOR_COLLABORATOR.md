The repo's own pin (transformers 4.52.4) does not work: 4.52.4 lacks `CacheLayerMixin`, 4.56 lacks `is_initialized` on cache layers, 5.x lacks the `default` rope entry; 4.57.1 works.
- Env vars: `MASK_TOKEN_ID=126336 EOS_TOKEN_ID=126081 PAD_TOKEN_ID=126081` (LLaDA); `HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1 TRANSFORMERS_OFFLINE=1`.
- We bypass the repo's `eval.py` / hydra / lm-eval and call `src.generation.generate(...)` from `run_d2c.py` with our prompts. What we actually ran: `strategy="vanilla"`, `threshold=0.9`, `num_transfer_tokens=1`, `block_length=32`, `temperature=0.0`, `stop_until_eos=False`, `alg="maskgit_plus"`, with `cache_cls` = none (dense anchor, sdpa), `PrefixCache(use_dual=True)` (Fast-dLLM dual), `dLLMCache(kr=5, kp=25, rou=0.25)`.
- **KLASS and EB-Sampler were NOT run by us.** They come from the same repo (`strategy="klass"` with `kl_threshold`, `kl_history_length`; `strategy="eb_sampler"` with `gamma`; configs in `configs/generation/`), but our driver was only exercised with `vanilla`, so expect to check argument names and the cache combinations yourself. The `d2cache` cache arm failed in our driver (`assert self.active_q_mask is not None` in `src/cache/d2cache.py:166`, with and without `sigma`), so we dropped it.
- Numerics: the d2Cache stack's dense Conf-0.9 decode (sdpa) matched our engine's on 12 of 20 HumanEval prompts (first EOS), calls within 7%; speeds are only comparable inside a stack, so run each stack's own dense anchor.
