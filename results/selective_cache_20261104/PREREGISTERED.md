# Pre-registration: selective K/V caching for LLaDA-8B-Instruct (2026-11-04)

Written BEFORE any GPU decode of this campaign (the CPU checks on tiny models and toy stacks were run before; they say nothing about LLaDA-8B). Part C gets its own addendum below, written after Part A and the parity gate and before any Part C decode.

Words used. WORD LOCK = which blank (masked) positions get a token written in a call (saves calls). REPRESENTATION FREEZE / cache = which rows have their K/V cached and skipped (saves compute per call). This campaign tests only the second; the word lock is either our dense Confidence-0.9 (block 32, Part A) or Elastic-Cache's own (Confidence-0.9 inside a window of 16 blanks, Part B/C). Row types: prompt rows, written answer rows, blank rows (in the window / beyond the window inside the current 32-block / in later blocks). Dataset: MATH (MATH-500 questions), generation lengths 256 and 512.

## Deviation from the brief, decided before any decode (Part A questions)
The requested "MATH train split used for gate collection" (collect_mc `math_train`, 134 questions) is not clean: its 54 L1-4 questions ARE the held-out `longlen_wordlock` `math_l14` set (Part C evaluates on them) and 36 of its 80 L5 questions are the Test-4 fresh set. Only 44 L5 questions and no L1-4 question of that split are free. The 40 Part A questions are therefore: 20 MATH L5 drawn (seed 23) from the 44 free L5 questions of that split, and 20 MATH L1-4 drawn (seed 23) from the 312 MATH-500 L1-4 questions that appear in no manifest (longlen 108, Test-4 141, compare panel 124, collect_mc report, `math500_levels1to4_54_seed23`). `manifests/math40_train.jsonl`, `manifests/audit_A.json` (overlap with every excluded pool = 0). Every threshold or rule used later is fit on these 40 questions only.

## Part A: does exposure predict K/V change?
Data. Dense Confidence-0.9 decodes (block 32, nothing cached or skipped), 40 questions x {256, 512} tokens. At every call, for every layer, relative L2 change of every row's K and V (post-RoPE K, all heads concatenated, K and V concatenated) against the same row s calls earlier, s in {1,2,4,8,16}, valid only when the row holds the same input token at both calls (a row whose blank was just replaced by a word has no comparable snapshot and Elastic recomputes it anyway). "Stale" = relative L2 > 0.05 or > 0.10. Signals, defined in `orchestration/partA_capture.py` docstring: age; row class; exposure with the fresh query (E_fresh) and with the CACHED query (E_cq, what a cache that stores q can compute without recomputing the row), both accumulated over the calls since the snapshot; window mass on the row (accumulated and this call); distance to the nearest position written since the snapshot; Elastic's trigger cosine across heads (as coded; it is `cosine_similarity(a, b, dim=1)` on a (batch, heads, tracked, keys) tensor, so across the 32 heads at each key, then the mean over rows and keys) and across keys, per call and layer. Layer groups 0-10, 11-21, 22-31. Noise floor from an identical repeated dense forward and from K/V of a random 64-row subset recomputed from the same layer inputs.
Reading rules (set before the data). (1) A signal "predicts" staleness if its AUROC is >= 0.60 with the 95% interval (bootstrap over questions, 100 resamples) above 0.55. (2) "Exposure beats age (distance, window mass)" iff the paired bootstrap interval of AUROC(E_cq) - AUROC(competitor) lies above +0.02 for the all-rows pooled cell in at least two of the three layer groups at threshold 0.10; "ties" if the interval contains 0; otherwise it loses. (3) Elastic's tracked-row probe "tracks global drift" iff the Spearman correlation between 1 - cos (per call, per layer) and the share of stale rows at the layers above it is >= 0.40 for the as-coded (heads) version at age 1. (4) Within-age AUROC (age held fixed) is reported beside the pooled one because accumulated exposure grows with age by construction.
Predictions (point; range = my 80% interval). P1 identical repeated forward: max abs K/V difference exactly 0 (95%). P2 row-subset recompute noise: mean relative L2 0.002 [0.0005, 0.006], p99 < 0.02 (so 0.05 and 0.10 are at least 2.5x the p99). P3 drift grows with depth: median age-1 drift in layers 22-31 at least 3x that in layers 0-10 (80%). P4 share of valid pairs with drift > 0.10 at age 1, layers 22-31: prompt rows 4% [1, 15], written rows 12% [3, 35], blanks in the window 30% [8, 60], blanks beyond the window 8% [2, 25], blanks in later blocks 6% [1, 20]; at age 16 every class >= 2x its age-1 share (90%). P5 pooled AUROC at drift > 0.10, layers 22-31: age 0.68 [0.58, 0.78], distance 0.70 [0.58, 0.82], window mass 0.62 [0.52, 0.75], E_fresh 0.78 [0.65, 0.88], E_cq 0.74 [0.62, 0.86], Elastic cosine (heads, as a row score) 0.54 [0.48, 0.62]. P6 reading (2): exposure beats age 70%, beats distance 50%, beats window mass 70%. P7 held-out logistic: adding E_cq to class + age raises the 5-fold AUROC by >= 0.02 (65%). P8 Elastic probe: Spearman(1-cos_heads, deeper stale share) at age 1 is 0.25 [0.0, 0.5], reading (3) holds 30%; the keys version correlates better than the heads version (55%). P9 the trigger fires (cosine < 0.9 at some layer, dense fresh-vs-fresh, age 1) on 55% [15, 95] of calls (the official counter `layer_recompute_fraction` averaged 0.60 over our 124-question comparison, so triggers are frequent). P10 exposure scored at one shallow layer (a = 8) predicts drift at layers 22-31 with AUROC within 0.05 of the same-layer score (60%).

## Part B: implementation and parity gate (pre-registered readings)
`src/regret_remasking/selective_cache.py`: window + far-blank cache, first-EOS truncation, adaptive layerwise trigger (`trigger_cos` heads | keys, gamma), our row-selective refresh, plus schedules every_call / block / none. CPU tests: `tests/test_selective_cache.py` (13 tests: every-call refresh with whole-answer window equals the dense forward; refresh from layer 0 equals dense; trigger forced / never fires; row-set bookkeeping; EOS truncation; far-blank cache off; exposure extremes) and `validation/test_parity_cpu.py` (our port against the OFFICIAL generator, call by call, on a tiny model built from the official modeling code).
GPU parity gate, 3 MATH train questions at gen 256: (a) our engine on the OFFICIAL model object with the manual bf16 softmax equals the official tokens and call log (query rows, tracked rows, trigger layer, written rows) on 3/3 questions (prediction 60%; if not, the first diverging call is reported and the cause is looked for, not papered over); (b) our engine on OUR HF model with the manual softmax equals the official tokens on >= 2/3 (45%); (c) with fused SDPA on >= 1/3 (35%). The official full-recompute baseline equals our `every_call` control on 3/3 (60%). Parity failures that are numerical near-ties are separated from logic failures by the call log: a logic failure is a difference in the query set, tracked rows or trigger layer at a call whose inputs were identical.

## Part C addendum (written before any Part C decode; Part A / parity outcomes not yet known when this text was written)
Question. Which ingredient of Elastic-Cache buys its speed: the window decode itself, the far-blank cache, first-EOS truncation, the adaptive trigger, and does our write-driven row-selective refresh do better than the trigger? REPRESENTATION FREEZE side; the word lock changes between cells and is named.
Set and protocol. Gen 512, MATH 54 = the stratified even-indexed half of the 108-question long-length set (27 L5 + 27 L1-4: `longlen_wordlock_20261103/manifests/math_l5.jsonl[::2]` + `math_l14.jsonl[::2]`, the same 54 as the representation agent's S1024), disjoint from the 40 Part A train questions. ONE process, one timed repeat, one warm-up decode per cell on the first question, cell order rotated per question, CUDA-synchronised timers, temperature 0, block 32, threshold 0.9. Speeds are ratios of summed decode seconds inside this process, paired bootstrap over questions (10,000 resamples). Answer counts: lost / gained / net with the same bootstrap; resolution about +-4 answers. All W16 cells use the fused SDPA kernel (as the dense cell does); the manual-softmax variant is only used in the parity gate.
Cells (`orchestration/run_partC.py`): Conf-0.9 (dense, our block-32 word lock); SPARSE-none (same word lock on the sparse runtime, nothing cached: runtime-overhead control); W16-dense (Elastic's word lock = Confidence-0.9 in a window of 16 blanks + first-EOS truncation, every row every call, in the selective_cache engine: separates the word-lock change from the cache); W16-cache-block (window + far-blank cache, dense refresh at every block start, no trigger, no truncation); W16-cache-block+EOS; W16-cache+EOS+trigger (gamma 0.9, heads cosine as coded, 1 tracked row); W16-cache+EOS+trigger-keys; W16-cache+EOS+selective-r60 / -r80 / -r90 (our row-selective refresh at three thresholds). Not run: Lctx, learned gates, FIX rules, trigger+selective combinations.
Our row-selective rule and its threshold (fit on the Part A train questions only, `orchestration/calibrate_theta.py`, frozen before Part C): rows considered = rows the engine would not recompute anyway (prompt, written, blank beyond the window, blank in later blocks); label = K/V drift > 0.10 (mean over layers 22-31); score = cached-query exposure E_cq accumulated since the row's last refresh, scored at ONE layer a (all heads); a = the smallest of {2,4,6,8,10,12,16} whose AUROC is within 0.02 of the best; theta_rXX = the threshold above which XX% of the stale rows lie (XX = 60, 80, 90), fit on the gen-512 Part A data. A row above theta rejoins the computation at layer a + 1 (from its cached layer input, upstream's semantics) and all deeper layers are recomputed for it; its exposure resets.
GPU budget. Projected ~1.8 h (sum over cells about 110 s per question x 54 plus loading and warm-ups); cap 6 h. Not shrunk.
Readings. A ladder step "buys speed" if the bootstrap interval of its speed ratio lies above 1.05, "has no resolved effect" if the interval contains 1, "costs speed" if it lies below 0.95. A cell is quality-preserving if net answers vs dense Conf-0.9 >= -3 (the point rule used in earlier campaigns; the interval is reported, and a cell whose net interval contains 0 is not called a loss). Our selective rule "beats the trigger" only if its speed interval vs the trigger cell lies above 1.00 AND net answers vs W16-dense are not lower by more than 3.
Predictions (point [80% range]). C1 SPARSE-none 0.915x vs Conf-0.9 [0.88, 0.96]. C2 W16-dense: calls 0.97x of Conf-0.9 [0.85, 1.05]; speed vs Conf-0.9 0.93x [0.82, 1.02] (engine overhead of row-by-row execution, no cache); net vs Conf-0.9 -2 [-8, +4]; changed text 90% [70, 100]. C3 W16-cache-block vs W16-dense: speed 2.0x [1.4, 3.0], calls 1.08x [0.97, 1.4]. C4 EOS truncation: calls -5% [-15, 0] and speed +5% [0, +20] vs W16-cache-block. C5 Elastic trigger vs block schedule (both with EOS): speed 0.80x [0.5, 1.10] (the official counter says it recomputes layers on most calls), P(trigger cell faster than block-schedule cell) 25%; calls ratio 1.00 [0.9, 1.15]. C6 trigger-keys vs trigger: speed 1.0x [0.8, 1.3]. C7 selective-r80 vs trigger: speed 1.15x [0.8, 1.7]; vs block+EOS 0.9x [0.6, 1.2]; P(selective-r80 beats the trigger by the reading rule) 40%; refreshes per answer: selective-r60 > r80 > r90 (85%). C8 best cached cell vs Conf-0.9: 1.9x [1.3, 2.8]; P(any cached cell >= 1.3x) 90%; P(trigger cell is the fastest cached cell) 15%. C9 every cached cell net vs W16-dense within +-5 (80%); correct answers for dense Conf-0.9 on this set ~18 (S1024 gave 18/54 at 1024; at 512 about 15-20).

## Outcome (written after the runs; the text above was not changed)
Part A (512-token numbers; 256 agree). Held: P1 identical repeat exactly 0 (and the row-subset recompute is exactly 0 too); P3 deep-layer median drift >= 3x shallow (prompt 0.037 vs 0.005, blanks 0.15-0.37 vs 0.012-0.065); P4 "at age 16 every class >= 2x its age-1 share" (holds for layers 0-10 and prompt/written in 22-31; blanks are already ~90-100% at age 1, so the ratio test is vacuous there); P6 exposure beats age (+0.12 to +0.16, all groups) and window mass (two of three groups above +0.02); P7 class+age+E_cq vs class+age +0.024 (22-31); P9 fire rate 36% inside [15, 95]; P10 a=8 within 0.05 of the same-layer score: 0.766 vs 0.787 (held). Failed: P2 noise-floor point (0.002, range 0.0005-0.006; got exactly 0); P4 blank shares (window 91%, beyond-window 90%, later 66% vs predicted 30% / 8% / 6%) and prompt 18% (predicted 4% [1,15], just above), written 29% (12% [3,35], inside); P5 E_cq 0.787 (pred 0.74 [0.62, 0.86] inside), distance 0.754 (inside), age 0.659 (0.68 inside), window mass 0.740 (inside), Elastic cosine 0.651 (pred 0.54 [0.48, 0.62], above); P6 "exposure beats distance" (pred 50%): only in the deep group by the two-group rule: NOT met; P8 Elastic probe Spearman 0.55 (pred 0.25 [0, 0.5], above; reading (3) >= 0.40 holds, predicted 30%), keys better than heads (pred 55%): no (0.47 < 0.55).
Parity (Part B): (a) 3/3 held (pred 60%); (b) 3/3 held (pred >= 2/3, 45%); (c) SDPA 0/3 failed (pred >= 1/3, 35%); control every-call = official full recompute 3/3 held.
Part C. Held: C1 SPARSE-none 0.914x [0.912, 0.917]; C2 W16-dense calls 0.986x, speed 1.020x (pred 0.93 [0.82, 1.02], at the upper edge: the engine has no overhead), net +1, changed text 93%; C3 block cache vs W16-dense 1.84x [1.4, 3.0], calls 1.19x; C4 EOS: calls -6.7%, speed +7.1%; C5 trigger vs block+EOS 0.91x (not faster, as predicted at 75%), calls 0.93x; C6 trigger-keys vs trigger 0.97x; C7 selective-r80 vs trigger 1.07x [0.8, 1.7], vs block+EOS 0.98x; C8 best cell 2.04x, any >= 1.3x yes, trigger not the fastest; C9 every net vs W16-dense within [-2, +4]; dense Conf-0.9 correct 15 (range 15-20, at the edge).
